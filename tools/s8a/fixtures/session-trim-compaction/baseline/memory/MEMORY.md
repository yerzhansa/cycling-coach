## Recurring Coaching Agreements (established across check-ins, 1998-07-06)

### Pacing — Rolling/Climbing Sections
- Hold steady endurance effort on climbs; no surging (coach, 1998-07-06)
- Keep breathing conversational and cadence comfortable through middle hours of ride (coach, 1998-07-06)
- Final riser has felt harder than usual on multiple occasions; attributed to headwind + fatigue from long day on feet, not fitness decline (athlete, 1998-07-06)

### Wind / Position
- On exposed river stretch, tuck lower on bars when wind picks up (coach, 1998-07-06)
- Rotate snack schedule earlier in windy conditions to keep energy stable across final hour (coach, 1998-07-06)
- Monitor hands and shoulders for comfort after longer stints in lower position; comfort on bike is first thing to slip when weekly volume steps up (coach, 1998-07-06)

### Back-to-Back Ride Recovery
- Mild leg heaviness on day 2 of back-to-back rides is normal at this point in the build; clears after ~20 min of easy spinning (coach, 1998-07-06)
- Plan stays as written unless heaviness lingers past 30 min, in which case shorten day 2 rather than push through (coach, 1998-07-06)

### Fueling Protocol (rides over 2 hours)
- 1 bottle per hour (coach, 1998-07-06)
- A bite of something every 30 minutes (coach, 1998-07-06)
- Slightly bigger breakfast on mornings with sustained climbing work (coach, 1998-07-06)
- Practice fueling routine on easy days first so it is automatic on longer weekend rides (coach, 1998-07-06)

### Recovery Spin Routing
- Recovery spins start from the park side of town; fewer junctions makes it easier to keep effort genuinely easy (athlete + coach, 1998-07-06)
- Breathing staying relaxed throughout is the target signal for easy days (coach, 1998-07-06)
- Easy days to be protected even when schedule gets busy (coach, 1998-07-06)
