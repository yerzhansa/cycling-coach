## notes

- For rides over two hours: target one bottle per hour, eat a small amount every 30 minutes, and have a slightly larger breakfast before routes with sustained climbing (athlete/coach, 1998-07-06)
- Practice the fueling routine first on easy rides so eating on the bike is familiar before longer weekend rides (athlete/coach, 1998-07-06)
- In strong wind, move snacks earlier to stabilize energy through the final hour rather than fading late (athlete/coach, 1998-07-06)
